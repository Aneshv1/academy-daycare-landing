## Photos to Upload

Upload at least 20 photos. GBP listings with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks.

**Must-have photos:**
- [ ] Exterior/entrance of facility (so people recognize it when they arrive)
- [ ] Signage
- [ ] Indoor play areas (multiple angles)
- [ ] Outdoor yard
- [ ] Dogs playing in temperament-matched groups
- [ ] Staff working with dogs
- [ ] Rest area / cot area
- [ ] Treadmill / slatmill in use
- [ ] Drop-off area
- [ ] A mix of breeds and sizes

**Nice-to-have:**
- [ ] Before/after of a Day & Train dog
- [ ] Dogs on place cots
- [ ] Themed photo day shots (holiday, birthday)
- [ ] A short video walkthrough (GBP supports video)

**Photo tips:**
- Use high-quality, well-lit images (natural light if possible)
- Show real dogs, real staff, real facility — not stock photos
- Add geo-tags to photos before uploading (Google uses this for local ranking)
- Name files descriptively before uploading: "dog-daycare-gormley-indoor-play.jpg" not "IMG_4523.jpg"

## Q&A Section

Pre-populate your own Q&A section with common questions. You can ask and answer your own questions on GBP — this helps with AIO and featured snippets.

**Q: What vaccinations are required?**
A: All dogs must have current rabies, bordetella, and DHPP vaccinations. Dogs must be at least 16 weeks old.

**Q: Do you require an assessment?**
A: No. We don't require a multi-visit assessment process. Our canine behaviour specialists can handle any dog from day one — that's the benefit of 15 years of experience.

**Q: Can intact dogs attend?**
A: Yes. Spay/neuter is strongly recommended but not required. Intact males over 1 year often do best in our Day & Train program where they receive individualized management.

**Q: What are your hours?**
A: Monday–Friday. Drop-off: 7:00–10:00 AM. Pickup: 3:00–8:00 PM.

**Q: Do you offer tours?**
A: We don't offer facility tours as they're disruptive to dogs in session. We're happy to answer any questions by phone at 437-776-9563.

**Q: What's the difference between standard and behavioural daycare?**
A: Standard daycare is for well-socialized dogs that thrive in group play ($55/day). Behavioural daycare is for reactive, nervous, or high-energy dogs that need smaller groups and hands-on trainer management ($95/day).
