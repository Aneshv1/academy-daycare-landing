A lot of people ask what Day & Train actually looks like. Fair question — you're paying $95/day. Here's exactly what your dog's day includes.

A Day & Train day, hour by hour:

7:00–10:00 AM — Arrival & Settle
Drop-off window. Your dog arrives, decompresses, and transitions into the facility. Quick check-in with the team on any notes from home.
10:00 AM — Training Session 1: Obedience
Sit, down, stay, recall, heel. Foundation commands drilled with distractions — other dogs, sounds, movement. Not in a quiet room. In the real world.
11:00 AM — Structured Play
Temperament-matched group play. Social skills, appropriate play styles, and learning to disengage when things get too intense.
12:00 PM — Training Session 2: Leash Work
Loose-leash walking, heel, direction changes, ignoring distractions. The skill most owners struggle with the most — practiced daily here.
1:00 PM — Rest Period
Structured downtime. Crate or place command. Dogs learn to settle — this is training too. Most dogs never learn how to just be calm.
2:30 PM — Training Session 3: Place & Impulse Control
Place command with duration, door manners, waiting for food, ignoring triggers. The skills that make a dog livable at home.
3:00–8:00 PM — Play & Pickup
Afternoon play session, then ready for pickup. You get a tired, trained dog back.

That's three training sessions, two play sessions, and structured rest — every single day. A private trainer gives you one session a week. Day & Train gives your dog 15+ sessions a week in a real-world environment.
