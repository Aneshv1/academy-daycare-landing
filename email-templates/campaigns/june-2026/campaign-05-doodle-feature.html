We love Doodles. Goldendoodles, Labradoodles, Bernedoodles — we see them all, every single day. They're sweet, they're smart, and they're usually complete chaos.

If you own a Doodle, none of this will surprise you:

Energy that never ends. A walk doesn't cut it. Two walks don't cut it. They need to run, play, and wrestle with other dogs for hours before they even start to settle.
Mouthy and jumpy. They love people a little too much. Every guest gets the full Doodle welcome — jumping, mouthing, 60 pounds of enthusiasm landing on your chest.
Selective hearing. They're smart enough to know what you're asking. They just don't always care. Recall? Only when there's nothing better to do.
Zero off switch. They go from zero to 100 instantly and don't know how to come back down without help.

Here's the thing about Doodles — most of these "problems" aren't behavioural issues. They're energy issues. A Doodle that attends structured daycare regularly is a completely different dog at home. The energy gets channelled. The socialization teaches manners. The structured rest cycles teach them how to actually calm down.

We've seen Doodles go from "my house is destroyed every day" to "he sleeps on the couch all evening." The difference isn't training alone — it's adequate physical and mental stimulation, consistently.
