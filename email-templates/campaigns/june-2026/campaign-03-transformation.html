We see this more than you'd think.

A German Shepherd. Young, high-drive, not aggressive — just reactive. Lunging at other dogs on leash. Barking at everything that moved. Her owners had tried two different daycares. Both asked them not to come back.

By the time they called us, they were running out of options. They both worked full-time. Leaving her home alone made the reactivity worse. They were starting to wonder if they'd made a mistake getting a Shepherd at all.

We started her in Behavioural Daycare. Small group. Controlled introductions. She wasn't thrown into a room with 30 dogs and expected to figure it out — that's what the other places did, and that's why she failed.

Week one was about decompression. Structured rest. Learning that other dogs nearby didn't mean a fight was coming.

By week three, she was doing parallel walks with a calm dog. By week six, she was in a small play group. By month three, she transitioned to standard daycare full-time.

Today she has a pack. She has friends she plays with every day. Her owners stopped dreading the phone ringing during work.

This is what we do. Not just watching dogs — understanding them. We have 15 years of experience in behaviour modification. We've worked with dogs that other facilities won't touch. And we have a perfect safety record doing it.
