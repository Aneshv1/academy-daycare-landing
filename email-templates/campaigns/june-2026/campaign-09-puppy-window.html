If you have a puppy under 6 months old, this is the most important email we'll ever send you.

Your puppy has a socialization window. It starts at about 3 weeks old and the critical period closes around 14-16 weeks. After that, there's a secondary window up to about 6 months. Then it narrows significantly.

During this window, your puppy's brain is wired to accept new experiences — other dogs, new people, sounds, environments, handling. Everything they're exposed to positively during this period becomes "normal" for them as an adult dog.

Everything they miss becomes something to fear.

What happens when puppies miss the socialization window:

Fear of other dogs — leads to reactivity, lunging, barking on walks
Fear of new people — growling at guests, hiding, defensive biting
Poor social skills — doesn't know how to play appropriately, escalates every interaction
Generalized anxiety — a dog that's stressed by the world around them, for the rest of their life

Fixing these problems in an adult dog costs thousands of dollars, takes months of behaviour modification work, and never fully undoes the damage. We know — we do that work. And every time, the owner says the same thing: "I wish I'd started earlier."

Daycare during the socialization window is the single best investment you can make in your puppy's future. Daily exposure to other dogs, new people, sounds, and environments — all structured, all supervised, all positive. Your puppy learns that the world is a safe place. That skill lasts a lifetime.
