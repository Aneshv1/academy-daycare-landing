Most daycares open the door, let dogs loose in a room, and hope for the best. That's not what happens here.

At Academy, every day is structured — because dogs thrive on routine, not chaos. Here's what a typical day actually looks like inside our 8,500 sq ft facility:

Morning (7–10 AM) — Arrival & settling in

Dogs arrive during drop-off and get settled into their temperament-matched group. We observe every dog's energy and body language before play begins — no one gets thrown into the mix blind.

Late morning — Structured play

Play sessions are supervised and intentional. Dogs are grouped by temperament, size, and play style — not just tossed together by age or breed. Our team actively manages the room, redirecting when needed and reinforcing good social behaviour.

Midday — Rest & reset

Mandatory rest cycles. Dogs need downtime to decompress — overstimulation leads to bad behaviour and stress. This is one of the biggest things franchise daycares get wrong.

Afternoon — Outdoor time & second play session

Dogs rotate through outdoor time and a second structured play session. By pickup (3–8 PM), your dog has had a full, balanced day — and they'll sleep well tonight.

This matters because our team aren't just dog watchers. With 15 years of experience in behaviour modification and a perfect safety record, we understand why dogs act the way they do — and we manage the environment accordingly.

Need more than daycare?

If your dog could use some training alongside their daycare day, our Day & Train program ($95/day) combines structured socialization with one-on-one training sessions. Your dog gets daycare and comes home better behaved. It's the most efficient way to train — no extra trips, no separate schedule.

Have questions about how we'd handle your dog specifically? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through.
