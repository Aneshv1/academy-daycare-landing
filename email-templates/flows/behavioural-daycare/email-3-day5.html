When a dog is reactive or anxious, the natural instinct is to avoid the triggers. Skip the dog park. Cross the street. Keep them home. It feels like you're protecting them — and you are, in the moment.

But avoidance makes reactivity worse over time, not better. Every month without structured socialization makes the problem harder to address. The dog's world gets smaller. The outbursts get bigger. What starts as leash reactivity can progress to barrier frustration, resource guarding, or generalized anxiety.

We're not saying this to be pushy. We're saying it because we've seen what happens when owners wait a year to call us back. The dog that needed 4 weeks of Behavioural Daycare now needs 4 months.
