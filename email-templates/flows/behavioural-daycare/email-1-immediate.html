Thanks for reaching out about Behavioural Daycare. You're in the right place — this is exactly what we specialize in.

Behavioural Daycare is designed for dogs that don't fit into a standard daycare setting. That includes dogs with reactivity, anxiety, poor impulse control, over-arousal, or a history of being kicked out of other facilities. These are the dogs we're built for.

What Behavioural Daycare looks like:

Smaller groups with higher staff-to-dog ratios
Every session is trainer-led — staff are behaviour modification experts, not babysitters
Structured confidence building — this isn't isolation, it's controlled socialization
15 years of experience handling dogs that other facilities turn away
